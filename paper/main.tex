\title{Gonality and quintic points on intermediate modular curves}
\author{Marin Varivoda}
\address{Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Science, University of Zagreb, Bijeni\v{c}ka cesta 30, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia}
\email{marin.varivoda@math.hr}
\date{\today}
\subjclass[2020]{11G18, 14H51, 11G05, 14G05}
\keywords{modular curves, gonality, points of low degree, density degree}

\begin{abstract}
For $\{\pm1\}\subseteq\Delta\subseteq(\Z/N\Z)^\times$ let $X_\Delta(N)$ be the intermediate modular curve
between $X_1(N)$ and $X_0(N)$. We determine the $\Q$-gonality of every strict intermediate curve $X_\Delta(N)$
with $N\le 50$, and of $X_1(N)$ for $41\le N\le 50$, where it equals the upper bound of Derickx and van Hoeij
at every level. We also settle the thirty levels left open by Derickx, Hwang, Jeon and Orli\'c, completing the
list of curves $X_0(N)$ with infinitely many points of degree $5$, and we show that exactly $43$ strict
intermediate curves have infinitely many points of degree $5$. As a consequence, the strict intermediate curves
of density degree $5$ are exactly the four $\Q$-pentagonal curves found by Orli\'c. The proofs use published
classifications, the Castelnuovo--Severi inequality, and three computational methods; all computations can be
rerun from the accompanying code.
\end{abstract}
